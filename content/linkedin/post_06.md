# Post 6 — I'm not building a procurement tool.
**Publish:** Week 6
**Target reaction:** Recognition from the right people. CFOs, CPOs, and the procurement professionals who've been nodding for five weeks should feel directly addressed. This is the call.

---

I'm not building a procurement tool.

I'm building the answer to a problem that procurement was always trying to solve badly.

The problem is real. Trust and risk at scale. When a business is spending €100M a year across hundreds of suppliers and thousands of transactions, someone needs to ensure the spend is justified, the suppliers are performing, and the contracts protect the business. That problem doesn't go away. The answer we built — departments, committees, approval chains, taxonomies, scorecards — made complete sense when information was slow and reasoning was expensive. You needed humans to hold the complexity because nothing else could.

Information is now instant. Reasoning is now cheap. The answer we built is no longer the right answer to the problem.

There are three people this is for.

The CFO who has always wondered, quietly, what procurement is actually for and why it costs so much while the business still feels exposed.

The CPO who sees the transformation coming and wants to own it rather than be overtaken by it.

The procurement professional who knows, if they're honest, that their daily work shouldn't require a human being.

The product is called TrueSpend.

Three things need your attention this week. Everything else, the agent has already closed.

If any of this has landed: reach out.

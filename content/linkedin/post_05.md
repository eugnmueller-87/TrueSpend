# Post 5 — Imagine you run procurement for a company spending €70M a year on hardware.
**Publish:** Week 5
**Target reaction:** Recognition from people who live this. "This is my week" energy. Stakes made visceral.

---

Imagine you run procurement for a company spending €70M a year on hardware.

Dell, Lenovo, Apple. Three major renewals cycling on different schedules. AWS, GCP, and Azure burning continuously — committed spend tracked in three separate spreadsheets, none of them current. Hundreds of SaaS contracts on rolling cycles that nobody fully tracks. Right now, somewhere in your organisation, someone is chasing an approval. Someone else is filling out a supplier onboarding form. Someone is putting together a renewal brief that is three weeks late.

None of that is the job.

The job is: are we getting the best commercial position on €120M of annual spend, across suppliers who are performing, on contracts that actually protect us? Five signals, simultaneously. Contract status. Consumption patterns. Supplier health. Request patterns from the business. Current policy. You need to hold all five, reason across them in real time, and surface what needs attention before it becomes urgent.

Nobody on your team is holding all five signals. They're holding whichever one is loudest that week.

The people on that team should be having two conversations a year with the Dell account director. Fully prepared. Commercially focused. That conversation is worth real money. The three weeks spent preparing an approval nobody wanted to give is not.

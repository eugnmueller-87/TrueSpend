# TRUESPEND — The Fall of Procurement

![TrueSpend](screenshots/hero.png)

**Procurement doesn't need to be faster. It needs to not exist.**

TrueSpend is an agentic procurement operating system. It reasons across contracts, consumption, supplier health, request patterns, and policy state — and closes transactions without human touch. The humans in the loop focus on the strategic supplier relationships where judgment actually matters.

---

## The Philosophy

Procurement exists because someone had to manage trust and risk at the intersection of buyer and supplier, at volume, without good information.

Information is now instant. Reasoning is now cheap.

The apparatus — the approval chains, the scorecards, the workflows, the committees — is no longer necessary.

TrueSpend doesn't automate procurement. It replaces the need for procurement as a processing function.

> "Three things need you this week. Everything else, the agent closed."

---

## What It Does

- **Contract renewal engine** — watches every expiry, prepares every brief, drives every renewal until a human decision is genuinely required
- **Jira intake + agent reasoning** — every procurement request reasoned across five signals, closed autonomously or escalated with a complete brief
- **Hyperscaler monitoring** — AWS/GCP/Azure burn vs commitment, daily, before the bill arrives
- **Supplier onboarding** — automated due diligence, one checkpoint, one decision
- **Email threading + SLA signal** — institutional memory that survives personnel changes

## Principles

- Every feature answers one of three questions: *Should we buy this? Are suppliers delivering? What leverage do we have?*
- Nothing gets built that doesn't change a human action
- One owner per decision — no CC chains, no committees
- The audit trail is the agent's thought process
- The agent earns its own authority expansion through demonstrated accuracy
